# PRODUCT — JustToBeSafe

> Product context for Impeccable and other coding agents. Provisional, validation-stage information only. Source of truth: `docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md` and `docs/PHASE_0_VALIDATION.md`.

## Identity and stage
**Working product name:** JustToBeSafe (brand/trademark clearance pending)  
**Stage:** Phase 0 — validate demand before substantial development  
**Platforms:** UK-first; Android-first MVP is a *proposal*, and framework choice is not settled.  
**Business model:** consumer freemium subscription hypothesis; no prices or subscription entitlements approved.

## Audience
Ordinary UK households, including individuals and families who want a simple, trustworthy way to prepare for power cuts, floods, storms or other disruptions. Avoid framing users as survivalists or exploiting anxiety.

## Customer jobs to test
- Understand which household preparations are worth organising.
- Keep essential reference information available when connectivity is disrupted.
- Create a practical shared household plan and maintain a basic supplies inventory.
- Find credible, location-appropriate resources and know their review status.

These are **hypotheses**, not validated user needs.

## Phase 0 deliverables
A clear, accessible, mobile-first landing page; honest "not yet launched" status; an optional waitlist subject to privacy review; user interviews; and tests of value proposition and willingness to pay.

## Trust and safety boundaries
- No unverified emergency instructions or fabricated official endorsements.
- Essential emergency guidance and warnings stay accessible without a subscription.
- Reviewed and licensed source materials must remain distinguishable from ideas and placeholder copy.
- Never represent an offline article as a live alert.
- Avoid collecting personal, household or precise location data in demo fixtures or source control.

## Open decisions
Final brand, visual identity, pilot region, technical framework, pricing, expert partners, licensing rights and legal copy. Agents must not silently decide these.
