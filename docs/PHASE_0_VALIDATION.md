# Phase 0 — UK Market Validation Plan

**Goal:** Determine whether UK households want an offline-first preparedness product badly enough to become engaged, paying customers. This is a research plan, not proof of product-market fit.

## Hypotheses to test

| ID | Hypothesis | Evidence to gather |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Ordinary UK households experience a meaningful preparedness/organisation problem | Customer interviews about actual recent behaviour, not only stated worries |
| H2 | An offline household plan solves a problem beyond free official webpages | Task-based prototype feedback; comparisons to existing guidance |
| H3 | The proposed subscription features could justify paying regularly | Unprompted price expectations, honest price-page experiments, follow-through |
| H4 | Acquisition channels can recruit interested households outside emergency-news spikes | Source-tagged waitlist cohorts over several weeks |
| H5 | Safety, trust and data minimisation support adoption | Concerns around expert review, location and household data collection |

## Work packages

### 1. Competitive research
Identify UK official resources and direct/indirect preparedness competitors. Compare free coverage, offline availability, family plans, checklists, app stores, updates, and pricing using dated sources. Do not copy or reuse protected content during research.

### 2. Customer discovery
Recruit a diverse mix of UK household types. Ask about the last disruption they personally prepared for or experienced, what they did, what they forgot and whether existing options failed them. Avoid leading interviewees toward the proposed app. Record aggregate insights, not personal emergency details.

### 3. Landing page + waitlist
Build a mobile-first public page with:
- Clear headline and examples (power cuts, floods, storms).
- Proposed product outcomes rather than unverified safety claims.
- Honest product status: *not yet launched*.
- Optional email waitlist with explicit, understandable consent and a suitable privacy notice reviewed before launch.
- Source attribution for marketing examples if safety-related.
- Campaign/referrer tracking without invasive location data collection.
- An accessible form, clear confirmation and easy withdrawal/unsubscribe path.

Do **not** claim alerts are delivered or emergency content is expert approved before those systems exist.

### 4. Pricing experiment
Test founder-approved price hypotheses and plan packaging before implementing checkout. Make clear when a survey or fake-door experiment is **not** an actual purchase. Do not collect payments for unavailable features. Analyse whether interest translates into intentional next steps.

### 5. Evaluation
Report:
- Waitlist signups and where they came from (segmented by channel).
- Landing-page conversion denominator and rate.
- Interview themes and frequency of actual behaviours.
- Price-interest signals, with uncertainty stated.
- Evidence of repeat engagement or planned next action, where measurable.
- Acquisition cost estimates where spend occurred.
- Safety, content and legal feasibility issues that could block MVP.

**Initial ambition:** a few hundred genuine UK waitlist signups, consistent with the founder's brief; this is an ambition, not a validated launch threshold. Do not use one numeric metric alone to green-light development.

## Go / iterate / stop review

- **Go:** Clear unmet need, specific repeated use cases, credible willingness-to-pay evidence, plausible responsible content and privacy operations.
- **Iterate:** Users like the idea but cannot articulate an unmet need, or would not pay.
- **Stop/pivot:** No practical differentiation from free tools, insufficient credible monetisation, or safety/compliance burdens are disproportionate.

## Milestone checklist

- [ ] Founder approves provisional user personas and pilot-region research scope.
- [ ] Competitive landscape with dated source references.
- [ ] Interview guide and sample recruitment plan.
- [ ] Landing-page copy and design reviewed for safety and privacy claims.
- [ ] Waitlist collection and consent flows implemented and tested.
- [ ] Channel and pricing experiment plan approved.
- [ ] Validation report with explicit go/iterate/stop recommendation.

## Decision and release gates

- No final subscription pricing, legal wording or partnerships without founder approval.
- No medical, emergency-procedure or hazard-specific advice published without specialist review.
- No personal household or waitlist data in this public repository.
- No significant app build until Phase 0 evidence has been reviewed.
