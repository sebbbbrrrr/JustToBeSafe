# JustToBeSafe — Product Brief

**Working title:** JustToBeSafe (brand clearance pending)  
**Business model:** Freemium consumer subscription with potential later family/group and organisational plans  
**Primary platform / market:** UK Android; iOS before US expansion  
**Current phase:** 0 — validate demand before major engineering

## The problem

When a household faces a power cut, severe weather, flood or another disruption, critical information may be scattered, connectivity may be degraded, and family members may not share a practical plan. Free official information exists, but often requires the household to find, understand and organise it.

## Proposed customer value

Make preparedness manageable in normal life: organise a household plan, keep essentials up to date, and have reviewed guidance close at hand even when disconnected. The product must not exaggerate what it can predict, prevent or achieve.

## Target audience and rollout

1. UK households — initial test market; choose **one UK pilot region** before deciding geography-sensitive content.
2. US households — only after retention justifies localisation and an iOS launch.
3. Selected European countries — after country-specific expert, regulatory and content work.

## Intended core experience

**Free (proposed):**
- Verified basic regional preparedness and hazard guidance.
- Emergency contact sheet populated only from verified official sources.
- Basic local hazard information.
- Essential guidance available offline; do not paywall urgent safety instructions or official warnings.

**Paid (hypotheses for testing, not approved pricing or policy):**
- Enhanced downloadable regional maps, guides and checklists.
- Household planning features and coordination.
- Supply inventory, rotation and expiry reminders.
- Customisable household-specific preparedness checklists.
- Additional alert convenience where technically and operationally supportable, without paywalling official safety warnings.

**Pricing hypothesis to validate, not selected:** roughly £3–5 per month or £30–40 per year for UK customers; family/group plans may follow later.

## Pilot MVP scope — subject to Phase 0 outcome

- Android app for a single selected UK region.
- Reviewed, dated and sourced preparedness guidance.
- Basic offline availability and clear online/offline status.
- Essential emergency contacts sourced from official agencies.
- Simple household plan creation and retrieval.
- Minimal analytics respecting privacy choices.

**Not pilot MVP commitments:** large multi-country content library, location-triggered push alerts, advanced B2B administration, community features, affiliate storefronts, complex live mapping, iOS.

## Non-negotiable content & safety requirements

- All safety advice requires appropriately qualified expert review before publication.
- Every content record must preserve source, source URL/identifier, licence status, author/editor, review date, geographic scope and next review trigger.
- Verify source-specific reuse rights; do not assume third-party charities' guidance can be republished.
- No claims of guaranteed protection, rescue, alert delivery or replacement for emergency services.
- Flag medical, legal, fire, evacuation, hazardous-material and other hazard-specific procedures for specialist review.
- Distinguish *static offline instructions* from *live warnings* which depend on connectivity and upstream systems.

## Privacy and compliance workstreams

Prepare for legal review concerning UK privacy law, children's data if relevant, app-store subscriptions and cancellation, safety-content liability and later international rules. Collect the minimum personal/location data. The repository must not contain user household plans or waiting-list data.

## Commercial priorities

- Validate actual willingness to pay, not simply click-throughs and email signups.
- Maintain clear separation between editorial safety content and any affiliate revenue.
- Consider partnerships and B2B only after direct-consumer traction and founder approval.

## Pending founder decisions

Final name and trademark search; pilot region; price and subscription packaging; legal copy; expert and partnership commitments; final framework and budget split.
